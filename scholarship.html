Frank Nativi | Management Information Systems Student

Why I Should Be Selected as a Recipient

My passion for information technology began during my service in the United States Marine Corps, where I worked as a Data Systems Administrator (MOS 0671). In that role, I supported network systems, maintained domain environments, and helped ensure reliable communications for operational units. Working in a mission-critical environment taught me discipline, troubleshooting, and the importance of secure, dependable systems.

After my military service, I chose to pursue a Bachelor of Business Administration in Management Information Systems at Sam Houston State University because I wanted to combine technical knowledge with business problem-solving. I enjoy understanding how systems work, how organizations rely on technology, and how secure and efficient information systems can make a real difference.

To keep building my skills, I actively work on certifications and hands-on technical projects. I recently earned my CompTIA Security+ certification, which strengthened my knowledge of cybersecurity, risk management, network defense, and incident response. I also earned the Certiport IT Specialist HTML and CSS certification, which reflects my ability to build and structure websites using modern web standards.

In addition to coursework and certifications, I spend time building practical projects that help me grow as a technology professional. I maintain my own website, work on web development projects, and build lab environments where I can practice networking, system administration, and troubleshooting. These projects help me move beyond theory and apply what I learn in a real and meaningful way.

My goal after graduation is to work in cybersecurity, network administration, or information systems support. Ideally, I would like to work in a role where I can protect systems, solve technical problems, and continue developing into a strong IT professional. As a military veteran, I bring persistence, adaptability, and a strong work ethic to everything I do.

Receiving this scholarship would help me continue developing my technical skills while pursuing my degree and career goals. It would support my education, allow me to focus more on building projects and certifications, and bring me one step closer to a career where I can make a meaningful impact through technology.
