Using Military, Veteran, and Dependent Education Benefits at SHSU

This website is designed to help students at Sam Houston State University understand what education benefits they may use, who qualifies, and what steps to take to start using those benefits successfully.

Explore Benefits

Who This Guide Is For

This guide is for students at SHSU who are:

Veterans

Former service members using earned education benefits after military service.

Active Duty Military

Currently serving students who may use Tuition Assistance or VA education benefits.

Dependents

Spouses and children who may qualify for transferred benefits or dependent education programs.

Education Benefits Students May Use at SHSU

Post-9/11 GI Bill (Chapter 33)

Helps pay tuition and fees and may also provide a monthly housing allowance and book stipend. This is one of the most commonly used benefits for veterans and some dependents.

Montgomery GI Bill (Chapter 30)

Provides education payments directly to eligible students and can be used toward approved college programs and training.

Veteran Readiness & Employment (Chapter 31)

Supports eligible veterans with service-connected disabilities through education, job training, and career preparation.

Dependents' Educational Assistance (Chapter 35)

Available to certain spouses and dependents of veterans who are disabled or deceased due to service.

Selected Reserve GI Bill (Chapter 1606)

Supports members of the Selected Reserve who qualify for education assistance while serving.

Hazlewood Exemption

A Texas education benefit that may exempt qualified veterans and eligible dependents from certain tuition and fee charges at public institutions like SHSU.

Military Tuition Assistance

Active duty service members may use Tuition Assistance through their military branch to help pay for classes while enrolled at SHSU.

How to Start Using Benefits at SHSU

1

Apply for Your Benefit

First, apply for the benefit you plan to use through the VA, Texas Hazlewood program, or your military branch if using Tuition Assistance.

2

Get Your Approval Documents

Once approved, collect the required paperwork such as your Certificate of Eligibility, military paperwork, or Hazlewood documents.

3

Submit Documents to SHSU

Send your required documents to SHSU’s Veterans Benefits Processing office so the school can review and certify your enrollment.

4

Register for Classes

Enroll in approved courses that fit your degree plan. This is important because only approved coursework is typically covered by benefits.

5

Monitor Your Status

Stay in contact with SHSU and check your student account regularly to make sure your certification, payments, or exemptions are processed properly.

Admissions, Financial Aid, and Advising

Common Student Questions

Will all benefits pay the same way?

No. Some benefits pay tuition directly to the school, while others may pay the student directly.

Does Hazlewood pay for housing or books?

No. Hazlewood generally applies to eligible tuition and fee exemptions only.

Can dependents use benefits at SHSU?

Yes, if they qualify through a transferred GI Bill benefit, Chapter 35, or Hazlewood Legacy.

Do active duty students use the same process as veterans?

Not always. Active duty students may need approval from their military branch for Tuition Assistance first.
